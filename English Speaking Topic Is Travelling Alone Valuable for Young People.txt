Topic: Is Travelling Alone Valuable for Young People

Good morning everyone.

Today, I am going to talk about travelling alone and why it can be valuable for young people.

Firstly, travelling alone can teach young people how to make decisions.
 They have to plan their journey and think about what to do.
 For example, they may need to choose a hotel or decide which bus or train to take.

Also, travelling alone can build confidence.
 When young people do things by themselves, they learn how to deal with problems.
 For example, if they get lost, they can ask someone for directions.

Another good thing is that travelling can help young people learn about different cultures.
 They can meet new people, try different foods, and see new places.

However, travelling alone can also have some challenges.
 A young person may feel lonely or may face a problem in a new place.
 Because of this, it is important to plan the journey and stay safe.

Personally, I would like to travel alone when I am older.
 I would keep my phone with me, stay in safe places, and keep in contact with my family.

Finally, I think travelling alone can help young people become more independent
and learn useful skills for the future.

Thank you for listening.
