# Fractions

Fractions represent **parts of a whole**.

## 1. Parts of a Fraction

$$
\frac{3}{5}
$$

* **3** = Numerator → parts we have
* **5** = Denominator → total equal parts

---

## 2. Types of Fractions

### Proper Fraction

Numerator is smaller than denominator.

`3/5`

### Improper Fraction

Numerator is greater than or equal to denominator.

`7/5`

### Mixed Number

A whole number and a fraction.

`1 2/5`

---

## 3. Simplifying

Divide the numerator and denominator by the same number.

$$
\frac{8}{12}=\frac{8\div4}{12\div4}=\boxed{\frac23}
$$

---

## 4. Equivalent Fractions

Multiply or divide the top and bottom by the same number.

$$
\frac12=\frac24=\frac36=\frac48
$$

---

## 5. Adding Fractions

### Same Denominator

$$
\frac27+\frac37=\frac57
$$

### Different Denominators

$$
\frac12+\frac13
$$

Common denominator = 6

$$
\frac36+\frac26=\boxed{\frac56}
$$

---

## 6. Subtracting Fractions

$$
\frac56-\frac13
$$

$$
\frac56-\frac26=\boxed{\frac12}
$$

---

## 7. Multiplying Fractions

Multiply numerator × numerator and denominator × denominator.

$$
\frac23\times\frac45
=
\frac8{15}
$$

**Answer:** `8/15`

---

## 8. Dividing Fractions

Use **Keep, Change, Flip**.

$$
\frac23\div\frac45
$$

$$
\frac23\times\frac54
=
\boxed{\frac56}
$$

---

## 9. Mixed Numbers

Convert:

$$
2\frac13
$$

$$
(2\times3)+1=7
$$

$$
\boxed{\frac73}
$$

---

## 10. Fraction of an Amount

Find `3/5` of 40.

$$
40\div5=8
$$

$$
8\times3=\boxed{24}
$$

**Rule:**

`Amount ÷ denominator × numerator`

---

## 11. Fractions, Decimals & Percentages

$$
\frac12=0.5=50\%
$$

$$
\frac34=0.75=75\%
$$

To convert a fraction to a percentage:

$$
\frac34\times100=75\%
$$

---

## 12. Word Problem

A pizza has 12 equal slices. Sara eats `1/3`.

$$
12\div3=4
$$

Sara eats **4 slices**.

Slices remaining:

$$
12-4=\boxed8
$$

---

## ⭐ Quick Rules

| Operation          | Rule                       |
| ------------------ | -------------------------- |
| Add                | Common denominator         |
| Subtract           | Common denominator         |
| Multiply           | Top × top, bottom × bottom |
| Divide             | Keep, Change, Flip         |
| Simplify           | Divide by common factor    |
| Fraction → Decimal | Numerator ÷ denominator    |
| Fraction → %       | Fraction × 100             |
